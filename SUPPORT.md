# PadelOS Support

**Questions, problems or ideas?** Open an issue at
<https://github.com/Wrexist/PadelOS/issues> — describe what you did, what happened, and
your iPhone / Apple Watch models and iOS / watchOS versions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account or internet?** No. Everything works offline and stays on your
devices.

**The tournament doesn't appear on my Apple Watch.** Open PadelOS on your iPhone once
with the watch nearby and unlocked. On the iPhone, open the Watch app → My Watch →
PadelOS and make sure it is installed.

**I scored on the watch without my iPhone nearby.** That's fine. Points are saved on the
watch and sent automatically when the devices reconnect. The watch shows how many changes
are waiting.

**The score is wrong.** Open the match on the iPhone and use Undo, or Correct Result for a
finished match. Rounds that have already started are never changed.

**How do I change the language?** PadelOS follows your iPhone's language. To use a
different language just for PadelOS: Settings → Apps → PadelOS → Language.

**Privacy:** see the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
